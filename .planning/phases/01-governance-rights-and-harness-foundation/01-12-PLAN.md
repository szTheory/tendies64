rules and drives a runner skeleton that classifies every outcome correctly, **so that** no emulator code
lands before the rules that govern it exist and are proven to fail when broken.

<interfaces>
Claim block, in a scanned Markdown file: opened by a line that is exactly three backticks followed by `t64-claim`, and closed by a line of exactly three backticks. One `key: value` per line.
- Required keys: `build_id profile_id tier fixture_set manifest_digest pass total guest_fail boot_fail timeout missing harness_error skipped oracle_kind oracle_revision not_claimed statement`.
- Conditional: `emulator` and `emulator_revision` (emulator-differential); `scope` and `capture_provenance` (hardware-derived).
- Optional: `evidence` (a repo-relative t64.result suite path).
Blocks fenced `t64-claim-example` are documentation and are not checked.

Tier phrases: smoke → "smoke-tested"; regression-pinned → "regression-pinned"; unit-tested → "unit-tested"; self-checking-fixture → "passes self-checking fixtures"; emulator-differential → "agrees with {emulator} {emulator_revision}"; hardware-derived → "matches hardware-derived expectations within {scope}". The `synthetic` tier is never claimable.

Rendered statement (CONTEXT A-4): `Tendies64 {build_id} on profile {profile_id}: {phrase} for {fixture_set} @ {manifest_digest} ({pass}/{total}; missing {missing}, skipped {skipped}, timeout {timeout}, harness-error {harness_error}, guest-fail {guest_fail}, boot-fail {boot_fail}). Oracle: {oracle_kind} {oracle_revision}. Not claimed: {not_claimed}.`

`scripts/check-claims.sh [--files F...] [--selftest]`. Default input: every tracked or untracked-not-ignored `*.md` outside `.planning/` and outside `tests/seeded/claims/` (intentionally invalid seeds). Output `RULE <id> path:line: message`, a summary `claims: files=N blocks=B errors=E`, and `ok` when clean. Rules:
- C-BLOCK-SYNTAX.
- C-FIELD-MISSING, C-FIELD-UNKNOWN.
- C-TIER-ENUM.
- C-SYNTHETIC: tier synthetic, profile synthetic-stub, or a build ending `+synthetic`.
- C-ID-FORMAT: build_id `^[0-9]+\.[0-9]+\.[0-9]+(\+[0-9a-z.-]+)?$`, profile_id `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9-]*$` (at most 64), manifest_digest `^sha256:[0-9a-f]{64}$`, counts as non-negative integers.
- C-COUNTS: pass > total, or the components do not sum to total.
- C-ORACLE.
- C-STATEMENT.
- C-RESERVED-WORD: accurate, compatible or supports (ASCII case-insensitive, whole word) in a block that is not hardware-derived with a non-empty scope, or on any line outside a block that starts with `Tendies64 `.
- C-EVIDENCE-SYNTHETIC.
- C-EVIDENCE-MISMATCH.
- C-EVIDENCE-INVALID.
- C-ZERO-INPUT.
</interfaces>
