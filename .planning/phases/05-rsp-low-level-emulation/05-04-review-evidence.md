## Efficiency lesson

A quiet executor was interrupted despite productive durable artifacts. Subsequent
handoffs must inspect attributable artifact/commit progress before treating chat
silence as a stall. Reconcile and reuse completed work after interruption.

receipts and avoid rebuilding unchanged diagnostic source or repeating semantic
checks merely because provenance annotations change. Full phase code/security
reviews remain assigned to Plan10; this review does not substitute for them.
