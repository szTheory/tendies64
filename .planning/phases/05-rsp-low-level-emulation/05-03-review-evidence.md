## Identity-reader lesson

A metadata annotation can change an executable hash without changing the
core identity. That does not authorize trusting an arbitrary replacement.
The supplemental receipt binds exact bytes, known build commands and source
provenance; the reader's limited role is identity reporting. Preserve that
separation when the next plan builds an actual RSP execution adapter. Native
rebuilds alone do not justify recompiling unchanged diagnostic ROMs.
