## Deviations from Plan

None to the work itself. Recorded for transparency (no Rule 1 to 4 deviation arose):

- **Poll alignment is a modeling clarification, not a change.** The plan text speaks of "poll 1 neutral, poll 2 Right+A, poll 3 B, poll 4 Start" and of stamps at field midpoints. Modeling it from `docs/spec/vi.md` showed that the VI pending bit (INTR = 2) rises 3,973 ticks after each field start, so poll 1 happens right after the VI enable, not before it, and poll `p` happens near `B(p - 1)` plus up to 60,000 ticks. The spec therefore states: the frame after poll `p` is the packet at `B(p)` and an event stamped at the midpoint of field `j` is first seen by poll `j + 2`. This reproduces the plan's own selftest values (square at (58, 40) after poll 2, at (56, 40) after poll 3, slot 56, 40, 1).
- **A nominal origin.** The field boundaries need the VI enable tick, which only the guest decides. The oracle carries a nominal `T0 = 550,000` (open IPL3 boot cost plus a few thousand ticks; the research prototype's first packet at about 1.59M ticks agrees) and the 200,000-tick stamp margin makes the expectations insensitive to a real value within 100,000 ticks of it. Plan 12-03 must measure the real `T0`; a value outside the band is a change to `title-spec.md` TS-4 first (recorded in PIN-IMPACT).
- **Extra spec rules beyond the plan's list**, all needed so the oracle never has to time the audio queue or loop without bound: A press edges at least 3 polls apart, the last one at least 2 polls before Start, a run models at most 64 polls, `--emit-inputs` output must equal the committed scripts (a drifted script makes `--check` exit 2, not 1).
- **Extra selftest cases.** The plan listed five behaviors; the oracle carries 114 cases (strict grammar table with reasons, clamp, stick threshold, edge and gap rules, restore validation, tone shape, document promises, CLI exit codes).
- **Checks outside the plan's verify lists, run for safety:** `ctest -R '^(gov\.|harness\.|repo\.docs|repo\.test-inventory|repo\.spike)'` (41 names, all passed, 22.8 s real at load 5) because new files under `fixtures/` and a new CMake test can trip the provenance, rights and timeout scanners; they did not.
- `requirements-completed` is empty on purpose: API-06 needs the guest, the runner and the consumer runs, not only the oracle.
